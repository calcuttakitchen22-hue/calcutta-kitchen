# Sitemap

## Overview

This document defines the complete information architecture for the Calcutta Kitchen website.

The sitemap is the single source of truth for navigation, page hierarchy, breadcrumbs, and URL structure.

Navigation should remain simple and intuitive while allowing the website to scale to hundreds of recipes.

# Collections

Collections are curated groups of recipes.

They improve recipe discovery and SEO.

Each collection has authored editorial metadata and filters. Its landing page and
recipe membership are generated from those filters, so recipes are never copied
into collection content.

Examples include:

- Festive Recipes
- Beginner Recipes
- Under 30 Minutes
- One-Pot Meals
- Healthy Recipes
- Comfort Food
- Party Recipes
- Summer Specials
- Winter Specials
- Lunch Box Recipes
- Editor's Picks

Future collections may include:

- Durga Puja Recipes
- Poila Boishakh Recipes
- Eid Special Recipes
- Christmas Baking
- Air Fryer Recipes
- High Protein Recipes
- Budget Meals
- Date Night Recipes

Collections are not new content.
They are curated landing pages built from existing recipes.

# Navigation Principles

Navigation should prioritize recipe discovery.

Users should reach any recipe within three clicks.

Every page should provide clear paths to related recipes, categories, and collections.

Avoid deep navigation hierarchies.

Avoid duplicate content.

Avoid chronological blog navigation.

Navigation should remain stable even as hundreds of recipes are added.
