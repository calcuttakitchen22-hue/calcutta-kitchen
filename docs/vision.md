# Calcutta Kitchen Vision

## Overview

Calcutta Kitchen is a premium Bengali cooking brand focused on making traditional Bengali recipes, modern comfort food, desserts, and global cuisines approachable for home cooks.

The brand combines authentic recipes, practical cooking techniques, and cinematic storytelling to create an enjoyable cooking experience.

The website is the permanent digital home of Calcutta Kitchen. It is designed to help users quickly discover recipes, learn cooking techniques, and explore related content while supporting long-term SEO growth.

This website is **not** a personal blog, magazine, or news website. Every design and development decision should reinforce recipe discovery and learning.

---

# Mission

Help people cook confidently by providing authentic, easy-to-follow recipes and practical cooking knowledge.

---

# Vision

Become the most trusted online destination for Bengali and Indian home cooking, while expanding into global comfort food through high-quality recipes, educational content, and seamless recipe discovery.

The website should evolve into a structured cooking knowledge base that grows alongside the Calcutta Kitchen YouTube channel.

---

# Target Audience

Primary Age Group:

16–35 years

Primary Audience:

- Students
- Young professionals
- Newly married couples
- Home cooks
- Beginners
- Intermediate cooks
- Bengali food lovers
- People interested in Indian comfort food

User Characteristics:

- Mobile-first users
- Discover recipes through YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and Google Search
- Prefer step-by-step visual guidance
- Limited cooking experience
- Busy lifestyle
- Value practical, reliable recipes over restaurant-style complexity

# Primary Goals

The website exists to help users:

1. Discover recipes quickly.
2. Find recipes through search.
3. Explore related recipes.
4. Learn cooking techniques.
5. Watch the corresponding YouTube videos.
6. Return to discover more content.

Every page should encourage users to continue exploring instead of ending their journey after reading a single recipe.

---

# Success Metrics

The website should improve:

- Recipe discovery
- Average session duration
- Pages per session
- Organic search traffic
- Returning visitors
- YouTube traffic from website
- Internal navigation between recipes

---

# Design Philosophy

The website should prioritize usability over decoration.

Core principles:

- Content first
- Fast loading
- Minimal distractions
- Excellent readability
- Large, high-quality food photography
- Clear information hierarchy
- Simple navigation
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Accessible to all users
- SEO-friendly architecture

Users should be able to find a recipe within 15 seconds.

Every page should answer three questions immediately:

- What is this?
- Can I cook it?
- Where do I go next?

---

# User Experience Principles

Navigation should feel effortless.

Search should always be easily accessible.

Recipes should never be buried beneath chronological blog posts.

Collections and categories should guide users toward discovering more recipes naturally.

Every recipe page should recommend additional relevant recipes.

The website should encourage exploration rather than linear reading.

# Visual Identity

The website should follow the official Calcutta Kitchen Brand Book.

Primary Colors:

- Orange (#F24E07)
- Cream (#F8F5E6)
- Maroon (#700D1D)

Visual style:

- Warm
- Editorial
- Premium
- Clean
- Spacious
- Photography-first

Avoid excessive animations, gradients, glassmorphism, or decorative effects that distract from the food.

---

# Technical Philosophy

The website should remain:

- Fast
- Lightweight
- SEO-first
- Component-based
- Easily maintainable
- Markdown/CMS friendly
- Accessible (WCAG AA)
- Optimized for Core Web Vitals

Every component should be reusable wherever possible.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The website should gradually evolve from a recipe website into a comprehensive culinary knowledge platform.

Recipes should become interconnected through categories, collections, and related content, enabling users to discover recipes based on ingredients, cuisine, meal type, cooking style, and cooking series.

Over time, the website will serve as the structured knowledge base powering future AI experiences and intelligent recipe discovery while remaining simple, fast, and enjoyable to use.
