steps:
  - instruction: Wash the white peas thoroughly and soak them for 10–12 hours. Drain
      before cooking
  - instruction: Peel the potatoes and cut them into medium cubes
  - instruction: Crush the garlic, dice the onions, chop the tomato and slit the
      green chillies
  - instruction: Transfer the soaked peas and potatoes to a pressure cooker
  - instruction: Add salt and enough water to cover everything by about 2 inches
  - instruction: Pressure cook until both the peas and potatoes are perfectly cooked
  - instruction: Reserve some of the cooking liquid
  - instruction: Heat mustard oil until smoking and reduce to medium heat
  - instruction: Add the crushed garlic and fry until lightly golden and fragrant.
      Do not over-fry
  - instruction: Add the green chillies followed by the onions and cook until soft
      and lightly golden
  - instruction: Add the chopped tomato along with salt and sugar. Cook until the
      tomato completely melts
  - instruction: Add the ginger paste and cook until the raw smell disappears
  - instruction: Add turmeric powder, red chilli powder and Kashmiri red chilli
      powder. Add a little reserved cooking liquid and cook until the raw smell
      disappears
  - instruction: Add the boiled peas and potatoes with enough reserved cooking
      liquid to achieve a thick, spoon-coating consistency
  - instruction: Immediately cover the pan and cook for 5–6 minutes
  - instruction: Check the salt and sweetness and adjust if required
  - instruction: Finish with tamarind pulp and freshly roasted cumin powder. Simmer
      for 1 minute and serve hot
tips:
  - Fry the garlic only until fragrant to prevent bitterness
  - Do not use excess ginger as it can overpower the flavour
  - Cover immediately after adding the peas to trap the aroma
  - Add tamarind and roasted cumin only at the end for the freshest flavour
