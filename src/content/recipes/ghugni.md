steps:
  - title: Soak and prepare
    instruction: Soak the white peas for 10 to 12 hours, then drain them and cube the potatoes.
  - title: Cook the peas
    instruction: Pressure cook the peas and potatoes with salt and enough water until tender, reserving some cooking liquid.
  - title: Build the masala
    instruction: Heat mustard oil, fry the garlic until fragrant, then cook the green chillies, onions, tomato, and ginger paste until soft.
  - title: Finish the ghugni
    instruction: Add the spices with a little cooking liquid, then stir in the peas and potatoes and simmer until thick and spoon-coating.
  - title: Add the final flavours
    instruction: Adjust seasoning, then add tamarind pulp and roasted cumin powder before serving hot.
tips:
  - Fry the garlic only until fragrant to avoid bitterness.
  - Add tamarind and roasted cumin at the end for the freshest flavour.
