steps:
  - group: "Phase 1 — Preparation"
    instruction: "Wash the Gobindobhog rice gently and drain completely"
  - group: "Phase 1 — Preparation"
    instruction: "Mix the rice with ghee, turmeric powder and ginger paste"
  - group: "Phase 1 — Preparation"
    instruction: "Rest for 15–20 minutes"
  - group: "Phase 2 — Frying"
    instruction: "Heat the ghee and fry the cashews until lightly golden. Fry the raisins until they puff up. Remove and keep aside"
  - group: "Phase 2 — Frying"
    instruction: "In the same ghee add the bay leaves, cinnamon, green cardamoms and cloves. Fry until fragrant"
  - group: "Phase 2 — Frying"
    instruction: "Add the marinated rice and fry over medium heat until the grains become slightly translucent"
  - group: "Phase 3 — Cooking"
    instruction: "Pour in the hot water"
  - group: "Phase 3 — Cooking"
    instruction: "Add salt and bring to a boil"
  - group: "Phase 3 — Cooking"
    instruction: "Cover and cook over low heat"
  - group: "Phase 3 — Cooking"
    instruction: "When the rice is about 80% cooked, add the sugar and fold gently"
  - group: "Phase 3 — Cooking"
    instruction: "Return the fried cashews and raisins"
  - group: "Phase 3 — Cooking"
    instruction: "Cook until the rice is fully done"
  - group: "Phase 3 — Cooking"
    instruction: "Turn off the heat and let the pulao rest, covered, for 15 minutes"
  - group: "Phase 3 — Cooking"
    instruction: "Fluff gently before serving"
tips:
  - "Always drain the rice well before mixing with the ghee"
  - "Fry the rice patiently until slightly translucent for separate grains"
  - "Add sugar only when the rice is about 80% cooked"
  - "Rest the pulao for 15 minutes before fluffing"

    answer: "The rice may not have been fried enough before adding water"
  - question: "Why is sugar added later?"
    answer: "Adding sugar near the end helps the rice stay separate"
  - question: "Can I use Basmati rice?"
    answer: "Gobindobhog gives the authentic flavour, but Basmati can be substituted"
  - question: "Why should the pulao rest after cooking?"
    answer: "Resting allows the steam to redistribute and keeps the grains separate"
