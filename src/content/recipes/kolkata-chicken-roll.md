  - "Use chicken thigh instead of chicken breast for a juicier filling"
  - "The same base marinade allows a fair comparison between Tawa and Kathi Chicken"
  - "Roasted besan and cream help the Kathi Chicken stay juicy during roasting"
  - "Do not overcook the onion and capsicum—they should retain a slight crunch"

faq:
  - question: "Why use chicken thigh instead of chicken breast?"
    answer: "Chicken thigh stays juicier and is more forgiving during high-heat cooking."
  - question: "Why are there two marinades?"
    answer: "Both versions start with the same base marinade so the comparison focuses on the cooking method rather than different flavours."
  - question: "Can I skip the Kathi version?"
    answer: "Yes. The Tawa version is a complete recipe and is ideal for everyday cooking."
  - question: "Why dry-roast the paratha before frying?"
    answer: "It removes excess moisture from the dough, helping prevent a raw flour taste while improving the final texture."
  - question: "Can I roast the Kathi Chicken in an OTG?"
    answer: "Yes. Roast until lightly charred, then chop and finish with onion and capsicum on a hot tawa for the authentic roll-shop flavour."
