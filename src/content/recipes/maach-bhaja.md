ingredients:

  - name: "1 tsp salt"
  - name: "1–1.5 tsp turmeric powder"
  - name: "1 tbsp mustard oil (for marination)"
  - name: "1/2 cup mustard oil (for frying)"
  - name: "1 onion, thinly sliced (optional)"
  - name: "2–3 green chillies, slit (optional)"
steps:
  - instruction: "Wash and thoroughly drain the fish"
  - instruction: "Marinate with salt, turmeric powder, and mustard oil for 10–15 minutes"
  - instruction: "Heat a round-bottom kadai until lightly smoking"
  - instruction: "Add mustard oil and heat until shimmering"
  - instruction: "Spread oil around the kadai using a khunti"
  - instruction: "Slide the fish gently into the oil instead of dropping it"
  - instruction: "Fry on medium-high heat for 2–3 minutes on the first side"
  - instruction: "Flip only when the fish naturally releases from the surface"
  - instruction: "Fry the second side for another 2–3 minutes until golden"
  - instruction: "For the optional finish, remove most of the oil and fry onions and green chillies until golden"
  - instruction: "Add the fish back and fry until extra crispy"
  - instruction: "Serve hot with steamed rice and dal"
tips:
  - "Never fry fish in a cold kadai"
  - "Don't skip the mustard oil in the marinade"
  - "Always drain excess water before marinating"
  - "Slide the fish into the oil instead of dropping it"
  - "Keep the flame slightly high to avoid temperature drops"
  - "Let the fish release naturally before flipping"
  - "A round-bottom kadai requires less oil and gives better results"
