ingredients:
  - name: 500 g potatoes, peeled and cubed
  - name: 4 tbsp white poppy seeds (posto)
  - name: 2-3 green chillies
  - name: 3 tbsp mustard oil
  - name: 1/2 tsp kalonji
  - name: Salt to taste
  - name: 1/2 tsp sugar (optional)
  - name: Water as required
equipment:
  - name: Mixer grinder
  - name: Kadai
steps:
  - instruction: Soak poppy seeds for 30 minutes
  - instruction: Grind with green chillies into a smooth paste
  - instruction: Heat mustard oil and temper kalonji and green chilli
  - instruction: Add potatoes and sauté for 4–5 minutes
  - instruction: Add salt
  - instruction: Add posto paste and mix well
  - instruction: Keep stirring till the raw smell of poppy seeds go away
  - instruction: Add a splash of water if required
  - instruction: Cover and cook until potatoes are tender
  - instruction: Add sugar if using
  - instruction: Cook uncovered for 2–3 minutes until the posto coats the potatoes
tips:
  - A smooth posto paste gives the best texture
  - Do not overcook after adding the posto paste
  - Traditionally the dish is fairly dry
  - Fresh green chillies provide the characteristic aroma
faq:
  - question: Can I make it without mustard oil?
    answer: Yes, but mustard oil provides the traditional flavour
  - question: Why is my posto bitter?
    answer: Over-grinding or stale poppy seeds can cause bitterness
  - question: Can I store leftovers?
    answer: Refrigerate up to 2 days and reheat gently
