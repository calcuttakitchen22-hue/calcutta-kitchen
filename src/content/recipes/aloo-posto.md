steps:
  - title: Soak and grind the posto
    instruction: Soak the poppy seeds for 30 minutes, then grind them with green chillies into a smooth paste.
  - title: Temper the oil
    instruction: Heat mustard oil in a kadai and add kalonji and green chillies.
  - title: Cook the potatoes
    instruction: Add the potatoes and sauté for 4 to 5 minutes, then season with salt.
  - title: Add the posto paste
    instruction: Stir in the poppy seed paste and cook until its raw aroma disappears.
  - title: Finish gently
    instruction: Add a splash of water if needed, cover until the potatoes are tender, then finish uncovered until the posto coats them.
tips:
  - Grind the posto until very smooth for the creamiest texture.
  - Keep the dish fairly dry rather than turning it into a thin gravy.
faq:
  - question: Can I make aloo posto without mustard oil?
    answer: Yes, but mustard oil gives the dish its characteristic Bengali flavour.
  - question: Why does posto taste bitter?
    answer: Stale poppy seeds or over-grinding can make the paste taste bitter.
