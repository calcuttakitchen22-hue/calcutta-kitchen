tips:
  - "Char the tomatoes well to develop the smoky flavour of the chutney"
  - "Add the garlic and ginger only after the tomatoes have started charring so they develop colour without burning"
  - "Add the coriander stems and green chillies towards the end as they need less cooking time"
  - "Let the charred ingredients cool before blending"
  - "Cook the blended chutney until the raw tomato flavour disappears and it reaches the desired consistency"
  - "Do not cook Kashmiri chilli powder in hot oil for too long as it can burn and turn bitter"
  - "Adjust the lemon juice at the end depending on the acidity of the tomatoes"
  - "The chutney will thicken slightly as it cools"
