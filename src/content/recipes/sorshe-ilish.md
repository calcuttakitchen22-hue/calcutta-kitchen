steps:
  - title: Marinate and fry
    instruction: Season the hilsa with salt, turmeric, and mustard oil. Lightly fry each side for 20 to 30 seconds.
  - title: Make the mustard paste
    instruction: Soak the mustard seeds, then grind them with green chillies and ice using short pulses. Strain the paste.
  - title: Prepare the poppy seed paste
    instruction: Use a little mustard-infused water to grind the poppy seeds, then mix them into the mustard paste.
  - title: Make the gravy
    instruction: Mix the mustard-poppy paste with turmeric, salt, warm water, and mustard oil.
  - title: Cook gently
    instruction: Temper mustard oil with kalonji and green chillies, add the gravy, then arrange the fish and cook covered over low heat.
  - title: Finish
    instruction: Add raw mustard oil and fresh green chillies, then rest for 5 minutes before serving.
tips:
  - Use ice while grinding mustard to reduce bitterness.
  - Do not boil the gravy vigorously after adding the fish.
faq:
  - question: Why use both white and black mustard?
    answer: White mustard adds body while black mustard gives the gravy its sharp flavour.
  - question: Why should the gravy not boil vigorously?
    answer: Excessive boiling can make mustard bitter and overcook the hilsa.
