  - group: "Prepare the Dough"

  - group: "Prepare the Dough"
    instruction: "Knead for 8–10 minutes until the dough becomes smooth and firm"
  - group: "Prepare the Dough"
    instruction: "Cover with a damp cloth or cling wrap and rest for 30 minutes"

    instruction: "Add the minced chicken, onion, garlic, ginger, coriander leaves, spring onion greens, Aromat powder, MSG and salt to a bowl"
  - group: "Prepare the Filling"
    instruction: "Add the softened butter"
  - group: "Prepare the Filling"
    instruction: "Mix vigorously for 1–2 minutes until the filling becomes slightly sticky and cohesive"
  - group: "Prepare the Filling"
    instruction: "If the mixture feels too tight, add 1–2 tsp cold water and mix again"
  - group: "Prepare the Filling"
    instruction: "Cover and refrigerate the filling for 15 minutes"

  - group: "Roll the Wrappers"

tips:
  - "Chicken thigh generally gives a juicier filling than chicken breast"
  - "Finely chop the onion instead of grating it so the filling retains some texture"
  - "The exact amount of water required for the dough depends on the flour, so add it gradually"
  - "The dough should be firmer than roti dough but softer than pasta dough"
  - "Rest the dough for 30 minutes to make rolling easier and reduce shrinkage"
  - "Mix the chicken filling vigorously until it becomes slightly sticky and cohesive; this helps produce a juicy filling that holds together during steaming"
  - "Add cold water to the filling only if it feels too tight or dry"
  - "Keep the centre of each wrapper slightly thicker than the edges"
  - "Start steaming only after the water reaches a vigorous boil"
  - "Do not over-steam the momos, as prolonged steaming can dry out the chicken filling"
  - "Freshly steamed momos are best served immediately"
faq:
  - question: "Why should the momo filling be mixed until sticky?"
    answer: "Mixing develops a cohesive filling that holds together during steaming and helps the chicken remain juicy instead of becoming crumbly."
  - question: "Can I use chicken breast instead of chicken thigh?"
    answer: "Yes, but chicken thigh generally produces a juicier filling. If using very lean chicken breast, you may need a little cold water to keep the filling moist."
  - question: "How thick should the momo wrappers be?"
    answer: "Keep the centre slightly thicker and roll the edges to around 1–1.5 mm so they are easier to pleat and seal."
  - question: "Why should the dough rest for 30 minutes?"
    answer: "Resting allows the dough to relax, making it easier to roll thinly without shrinking back."
  - question: "How long should chicken momos be steamed?"
    answer: "Steam them for approximately 8–10 minutes over vigorously boiling water. Avoid prolonged steaming because it can dry out the chicken filling."
