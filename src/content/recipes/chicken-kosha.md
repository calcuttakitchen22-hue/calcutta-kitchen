steps:
  - title: Marinate the chicken
    instruction: Mix the chicken with curd, turmeric, and salt. Rest for 30 minutes.
  - title: Build the base
    instruction: Heat mustard oil, add whole spices, then cook the onions slowly until deep golden and sticky.
  - title: Cook the aromatics
    instruction: Add ginger and garlic paste. Cook until the raw aroma disappears, then soften the tomatoes if using.
  - title: Bhuna the masala
    instruction: Add powdered spices mixed with a little water. Cook until the masala thickens and releases oil.
  - title: Kosha the chicken
    instruction: Add the chicken and cook over high heat, then continue on medium heat with small splashes of water until the masala coats each piece.
  - title: Finish and rest
    instruction: Cook until the chicken is tender, finish with garam masala and sugar, then rest for 5 to 10 minutes.
tips:
  - Cook the onions slowly; this stage builds the dish's characteristic depth.
  - Add water in small splashes during bhuna to keep the masala concentrated.
