steps:
  - title: Marinate and fry the fish
    instruction: Rub the fish with salt and turmeric, rest for 15 to 20 minutes, then lightly fry it in hot mustard oil.
  - title: Fry the potatoes
    instruction: Fry the potatoes in the same oil with a little salt and turmeric until lightly golden.
  - title: Build the spice base
    instruction: Add kalonji and green chillies, then add turmeric, red chilli powder, cumin powder, and a splash of water.
  - title: Make the broth
    instruction: Cook the tomato until soft, pour in hot water, and bring the broth to a gentle simmer.
  - title: Finish the jhol
    instruction: Cook the potatoes for 6 to 8 minutes, add the fish gently, and simmer until the jhol is thin and glossy.
tips:
  - Lightly fry the fish; it will continue cooking in the broth.
  - Add water immediately after the powdered spices to prevent them from burning.
faq:
  - question: Why should the fish not be fried until crisp?
    answer: It continues cooking in the jhol and should remain soft and tender.
  - question: How thin should the jhol be?
    answer: It should mix easily with rice while retaining enough body and flavour.
